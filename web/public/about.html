About ResumeRanker

Bridging the gap between state-of-the-art AI models and modern Applicant Tracking Systems.

Our Mission

We believe that evaluating candidates shouldn't rely on basic keyword matching or rigid legacy systems that overlook human potential.

Our platform leverages advanced Natural Language Processing to contextually understand candidate experience, drastically reducing time-to-hire while helping teams discover the best talent without inherent human biases.

Why We Built This

Recruiting is fundamentally broken. Standard ATS software parses resumes by blindly looking for exact text matches, discarding highly qualified candidates who simply used different terminology.

ResumeRanker replaces these outdated heuristics with true semantic understanding, enabling developers to build smarter hiring platforms and internal tools via our robust API.
