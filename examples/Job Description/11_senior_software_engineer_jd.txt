Senior Backend Engineer (Python/Go)

Job Summary:
We are seeking a highly skilled Senior Backend Engineer to join our Core Infrastructure team. You will play a pivotal role in designing, building, and scaling the backbone of our high-volume transactional systems. You will collaborate closely with product managers and frontend teams to deliver robust APIs and mentor junior members of the engineering org.

Key Responsibilities:
• Design, develop, and deploy scalable microservices using Python (FastAPI/Django) and Go.
• Architect robust data models and optimize queries in PostgreSQL.
• Drive the adoption of cloud-native patterns and manage containerized deployments via Kubernetes.
• Lead technical design discussions and conduct thorough code reviews to maintain high engineering standards.
• Troubleshoot and resolve complex performance bottlenecks in distributed systems.

Qualifications & Skills:
• 5+ years of professional software engineering experience, with a focus on backend systems.
• Deep expertise in Python; experience with Go is a significant plus.
• Strong understanding of relational databases, caching strategies, and event-driven architectures (e.g., Kafka, RabbitMQ).
• Hands-on experience with AWS, Docker, and Kubernetes.
• Excellent problem-solving skills and a proven track record of mentoring engineers.
• B.S. or M.S. in Computer Science or a related technical field.
