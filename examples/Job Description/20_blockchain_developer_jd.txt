Senior Smart Contract Engineer

Job Summary:
We are looking for a Senior Smart Contract Engineer to help build our next-generation decentralized finance protocol. You will be responsible for writing secure, highly optimized smart contracts that handle significant financial value.

Key Responsibilities:
• Design, develop, and deploy smart contracts on Ethereum and EVM-compatible chains using Solidity.
• Write comprehensive test suites (unit, integration, and fuzzing) using modern frameworks like Foundry or Hardhat.
• Architect decentralized protocols with a deep understanding of gas optimization and attack vectors (e.g., reentrancy, flash loan attacks).
• Work closely with frontend engineers to integrate smart contracts into web applications using Web3 libraries.
• Manage the smart contract audit process with external security firms.

Qualifications & Skills:
• 2+ years of production experience writing Solidity smart contracts.
• Strong understanding of the EVM, cryptography, and blockchain fundamentals.
• Experience with smart contract testing and deployment frameworks.
• Deep knowledge of DeFi protocols and common smart contract vulnerabilities.
• Solid software engineering background with experience in traditional backend development.
